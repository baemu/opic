# music2 OPIc Scripts


## 공통 암기 전략

- 음악 취향은 `rock and band music`으로 고정한다.
- 이유는 `music changes my mood` 하나로 반복한다.
- 습관은 `AirPods while moving around`로 통일한다.
- 라이브 음악 경험은 `concert with a friend`로 고정한다.
- 장비 문제는 `earphones stopped working -> warranty replacement`로 말한다.

## 만능 문장 묶음

- `Music is the easiest way to change the mood of my day.`
- `I usually listen to music when I am moving around.`
- `It helps me feel less bored and more relaxed.`
- `At first, I did not expect much, but it was much better than I thought.`
- `It was annoying at first, but luckily, the problem was solved.`
- `Since then, I check my earphones more carefully.`
