# inst2 OPIc Scripts

`5. inst.md`를 바탕으로 말하기 쉽고, 오픽 의도에 맞고, 암기량이 적도록 정리한 개선본입니다.

## 공통 암기 전략

- 악기는 전부 `electric guitar` 하나로 고정한다.
- 느낌은 `fun / difficult / calming / proud`만 반복한다.
- 루틴은 `practice alone at home -> simple chord or song -> short routine`으로 말한다.
- 과거 경험은 `At first, I was nervous -> luckily, it went well -> I still remember it` 흐름으로 말한다.
- 문제 해결은 `explain the problem -> ask for repair or replacement -> please let me know`로 통일한다.

## 만능 문장 묶음

- `The electric guitar is difficult, but that is why it feels fun.`
- `I usually practice alone at home for a short time.`
- `I do not try to practice perfectly. I just try to enjoy it.`
- `At first, I was nervous, but luckily, it went better than I expected.`
- `It was stressful at first, but the problem was solved in the end.`
- `Please let me know what option is best.`
