# family-house3 OPIc Scripts

`family-house2`를 바탕으로, `오픽_답변_준비법.md`와 `opic_script.md`의 원칙을 반영해 암기량을 줄인 버전입니다.

## 공통 암기 전략

- 모든 답변은 `main point -> feeling/reason -> detail -> short ending` 순서로 말한다.
- 질문이 넓으면 한 가지로 좁힌다: `That is a broad question, but let me focus on one thing.`
- 집/방 묘사는 `not fancy / simple / comfortable / peaceful`을 반복해서 쓴다.
- 루틴은 `When I feel tired, I just lie down and rest for a while.`를 중심으로 돌린다.
- 문제 경험은 `At first, I was worried, but luckily, the problem was solved.` 흐름으로 말한다.
- Role Play는 `I want to ask a few questions`, `Could you please`, `Please let me know`를 반복해서 쓴다.

## 만능 문장 묶음

- `It is not fancy, but it is comfortable and peaceful.`
- `It is not very big, but it has everything I need.`
- `When I feel tired, I just lie down and rest for a while.`
- `It is a small routine, but it helps me relax.`
- `At first, I was really worried, but luckily, the problem was solved.`
- `After that experience, I started checking things more carefully.`
- `To be honest, these chores are not always fun, but they are necessary.`
- `Please let me know when this can be done.`
