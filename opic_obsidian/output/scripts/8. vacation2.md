# vacation2 OPIc Scripts

`8. vacation.md`를 바탕으로 말하기 쉽고, 오픽 의도에 맞고, 암기량이 적도록 정리한 개선본입니다.

## 공통 암기 전략

- vacation은 전부 `slow break with family or close friends`로 묶는다.
- 활동은 `eat together / talk / walk / watch a performance` 정도만 반복한다.
- 과거 경험은 `not fancy, but satisfying`으로 마무리한다.
- 티켓/공연 Role Play는 `ask date/time/seats/payment`만 묻는다.
- 아파서 못 가는 문제는 `sorry -> sick -> use my ticket or reschedule`로 통일한다.

## 만능 문장 묶음

- `I do not need a fancy vacation. I just need time to relax.`
- `It was simple, but it helped me feel refreshed.`
- `What I like most is that I can enjoy small moments with people I like.`
- `I am really sorry, but I do not think I can go today.`
- `If that is difficult, we can choose another day.`
- `Please let me know what you prefer.`

## MP 문장 모음

### Set 1

- **Type 1:** I like spending my vacation with my family.
- **Type 2:** During vacation, I usually eat slowly with my family.
- **Type 3:** During my last school break, I spent a quiet day at home.
- **Type 4:** One time, I watched a live show during my vacation.
- **Type 5:** Hi, I want to know about your vacations. Who do you usually spend your vacation with?
- **Type 6:** Hello, I’d like to buy two tickets for tonight's show.
- **Type 7:** Hey, I’m sorry, but I can’t go to the show today.
- **Type 8:** I once missed a show because I got sick. I felt really sorry because I had to cancel.
